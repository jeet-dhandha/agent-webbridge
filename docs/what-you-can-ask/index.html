Ground rules
  • Replace anything in <angle brackets>. Keep the “stop before you submit” part for anything that sends, buys, posts or deletes.
  • The AI acts as you in your own Chrome. Watch the first few runs on a new task.
  • “Shown in demo” means we have a recorded run on the home page. “Recipe” means a documented procedure; “Basic capability” means a built-in tool (read, screenshot, PDF) without its own recording.
Not supported (yet)
  • Record my clicks and replay them: there is no recorder. Describe the task, or use a recipe.
  • Running on a schedule while you are away: not built in.
  • Anything that breaks a site's rules, such as bulk posting, fake accounts or buying limited items before other people: do not use it for that.
  • Chat windows that cannot run commands or connect MCP servers: they cannot set it up.
